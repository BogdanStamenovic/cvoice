Yesterday I read about a man who walked through the forest above the gorge.
He carried a rucksack, a jumper, and a photograph of his daughter.
She had stayed at home, angry that she was not going with him.
He crossed the first ridge before noon, and then the fog came down, thick and cold.
He called out, but nobody answered.
He got back late in the evening, soaked through, and said he would bring her next time.
